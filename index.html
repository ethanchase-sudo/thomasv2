The Book

A Second Chance

A powerful story that chronicles Thomas's journey from the brink of death to a life of meaning and service.

On Friday, April 13, 1990, Thomas Smith's life changed forever. What began as an ordinary night at a youth center dance ended in gunfire, chaos, and a fight for survival.

From a hospital room where doctors gave little hope, to the painstaking journey of relearning to walk, talk, read, and write, Thomas refused to surrender. Through unwavering faith in God, the support of family and friends, and a determination forged in adversity, he defied the odds. He graduated high school, earned a college degree, and transformed his pain into purpose — becoming an educator and advocate for students with disabilities.

Family & Roots

The people
who held him up.

SonBrotherFatherGrandfather

Thomas Smith was born and raised in Detroit, Michigan — and the city, his faith, and his family shaped everything that came after.

He is the son of the late Yvonne Smith and Thomas Barber, and grew up in a loving family with his brothers Darren, Anthony, and Donald, and his sister Danielle.

A devoted father and grandfather, Thomas cherishes his daughters, sons, and grandchildren, and honors the memory of his son, De'Terrious Jerome Hayes. His extended family, including cousins, friends, and loved ones, continues to be a strong source of inspiration and purpose in his life.

His upbringing in Detroit shaped his character, faith, and determination — and it was that same circle of family who stood by him through the hospital room, the relearning, and everything that followed.
